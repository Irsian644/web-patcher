# TheWebPatcher

**Register:** brand (design IS the product — a premium agency landing page)

## What it is
Landing page for TheWebPatcher, an Instagram-based web agency (`@thewebpatcher`) that builds fast, conversion-focused websites for small/local businesses (Albania + Europe). The site itself is the agency's primary proof of skill — it must look like it was made by a $50k design studio.

## Audience
Small business owners who currently rely only on Instagram. Non-technical. The site must make them feel "these people are premium, they understand design, I trust them" within seconds.

## Voice
Sharp · confident · expensive. Slightly aggressive but professional. Cinematic, not chatty. Apple/Linear/Stripe/Locomotive/Stink Studios altitude.

## Design direction (locked)
- **Lane:** Cinematic dark + oversized editorial typography. Near-black, dramatic whitespace, huge weight-contrast headlines. Monochrome with electric blue used *surgically* — never as ambient blobs.
- **Portfolio:** interactive drag before/after slider (Instagram-only → premium live site) as the standout moment.
- Fewer, heavier sections. Storytelling over feature lists.
